How Ordering Works

  • 1. Browse the shop and select the cards you want.
  • 2. Hit "Request Order" and fill in your name, email, and shipping address.
  • 3. We confirm availability and send you a payment link — usually within 24 hours.
  • 4. Cards ship once payment clears. Small orders go USPS First Class. Larger orders ship UPS Ground with tracking.

Condition Policy

All cards are Lightly Played to Moderately Played (LP–MP) unless a listing says otherwise. This means you may see minor edge wear, light scratches, or slight whitening — standard for played cards.

Our prices reflect condition. These are deck-ready cards, not PSA submission candidates. If you need NM, we probably can't help — but if you want to actually play, you're in the right place.
